# Diagonaut

**A chess-themed endless runner where you play as a lone pawn.**
Dodge the black army, capture diagonally, outlast the Black Queen and the Black King, and see how far a single pawn can go.

**[Play in your browser](https://kursuvat.github.io/Diagonaut/)**

---

## About

Diagonaut takes the most humble piece on the chessboard and sends it on a run. Black pieces march toward you down a 5-lane board. Hitting one head-on ends your run, but just like in real chess, you can capture anything that sits on your diagonal.

Every piece moves by its own rules. Knights mark the squares they threaten, rooks fire warning shots down their lane before they charge, and pawns sometimes arrive in protected chains where capturing the wrong one gets you taken right back.

The game is built for mobile but plays just as well on desktop, and runs entirely in the browser from a single HTML file. No install, no account.

## Game Modes

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | Endless run. Bosses appear, and you earn tokens from everything. |
| **Time Attack** | Score as high as you can in 60 seconds. No bosses. |
| **Dodge Only** | You can't capture, only dodge. Every piece that passes you is worth a point. |
| **Puzzle** | Handcrafted levels. Earn up to 3 stars on each. |

## How to Play

- **Phone:** swipe left or right to change lanes.
- **Computer:** use the arrow keys or A / D.
- Don't run into pieces in your lane head-on. Slip sideways or capture them diagonally.
- Pawns are worth **1** point, bishops and knights **3**, rooks **5**.

A built-in tutorial walks you through everything on your first launch.

## Bosses

In Classic mode, two bosses rise to stop you:

- **The Black Queen**, fast and relentless, attacking across wide zones of the board.
- **The Black King**, slower but far tougher, with heavier attacks.

Read their warnings, stay out of their attack zones, and wear them down for a big token reward.

## Power-Ups

Bubbles float onto the board during a run. Grab them for:

- **Shield** to survive a hit
- **Multiplier** for bonus points
- **Slow Time** to buy yourself room to think
- **Bomb** to clear the board
- **Extra Life** for a second chance
- **Tokens** straight into your pocket

## Progression

- **Levels and XP:** every match earns experience, and leveling up pays out tokens and loot chests.
- **Permanent upgrades:** spend tokens to make power-ups last longer, spawn more often, and more.
- **Market and cosmetics:** hats, glasses, capes, auras, trails, pets and alternative piece sets.
- **Loot chests:** bronze, silver and gold, each with its own rarity pool.
- **Achievements:** dozens of challenges across every mode.
- **Stats:** detailed records of your runs.
- **Secrets:** some things are hidden. Look closely.
